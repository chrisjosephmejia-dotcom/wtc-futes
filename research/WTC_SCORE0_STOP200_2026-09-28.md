# WTC V2 Score-0 Hysteresis — $200 Hard Stop Shadow Test

Date: 2026-09-28

Research only. No production strategy change.

## Setup

Baseline is the current WTC V2 score-zero hysteresis lifecycle: unchanged V1 entries, raw WAIT ignored while position score remains on the correct side of zero, long exits at score <= 0, short exits at score >= 0, immediate opposite full-signal reversal, and session-close flatten.

The shadow variant adds a $200 hard stop for 1 MNQ, equivalent to 100 NQ points adverse from entry. The stop is evaluated against subsequent 1-minute OHLC bars. If a bar opens beyond the stop, the model fills at the worse bar open; otherwise it fills at the stop level. After a stop, the normal 1-minute engine may re-enter at that bar close if the full entry signal remains actionable.

Robinhood Gold fees are modeled at $1.72 per completed round trip. Slippage is excluded except for the gap-through-stop rule above.
