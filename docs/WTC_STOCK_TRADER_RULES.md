# WTC Stock Trader — development rules

1. Never recommend a dip buy merely because a stock fell.
2. No BUY label unless live price is inside the displayed zone.
3. Only one final action may be shown at a time.
4. Unproven/newly public companies may still qualify for staged buys; the warning affects conviction and sizing.
5. A STRONG DIP must require a fresh thesis re-check before it becomes an actionable major add.
6. Technical price data should come from Tastytrade wherever possible.
7. Preserve the existing production site until the development build is tested.
