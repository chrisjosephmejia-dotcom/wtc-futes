LOCKED OPERATING RULES · V3
  • CME NQ normal hours: Sunday–Friday 5:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m. CT, with the daily 4:00–5:00 p.m. CT maintenance break. The first five minutes after each 5:00 p.m. reopen are blocked for new entries.
  • Entry logic is unchanged: trend-aligned setup needs at least ±5/9; mixed daily regime uses ±6/9; countertrend needs ±7/9, with 30m/15m/5m/1m alignment.
  • WAIT is no longer an automatic exit. Once long, hold through raw WAIT while master score stays above 0; exit when score reaches 0 or below. Once short, hold while score stays below 0; exit when score reaches 0 or above.
  • A full opposite BUY/SELL signal reverses immediately. Every V3 trade has a hard -25 NQ point stop (-$50 gross on 1 MNQ). Default take-profit is +300 points (+$600 gross); a short entered while the daily state is BULLISH uses +25 points (+$50 gross).
  • After a hard stop or take-profit, same-direction re-entry stays locked until the original V2 lifecycle would have ended: long lock clears at score <= 0 or an opposite SELL; short lock clears at score >= 0 or an opposite BUY.
  • Signals use direct NQ futures candles from tastytrade/DXLink. The displayed NQ price refreshes every 15 seconds, but strategy decisions and logging remain on the 1-minute engine. The live ledger assumes 1 MNQ and tracks Robinhood Gold fees at $0.86 per side / $1.72 round trip; slippage is not modeled.
