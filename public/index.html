LOCKED OPERATING RULES
  • CME NQ normal hours: Sunday–Friday 5:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m. CT, with the daily 4:00–5:00 p.m. CT maintenance break. The first five minutes after each 5:00 p.m. reopen are blocked; otherwise signals can fire throughout the open CME session.
  • Trend-aligned setup needs at least ±5/9; mixed daily regime uses ±6/9; countertrend needs ±7/9.
  • 30m, 15m, 5m and 1m must all agree. Closed market, stale data, conflict or extension = WAIT.
  • Signals use direct NQ futures candles from tastytrade/DXLink. Exit on a 1m reversal or 5m signal loss. Never average down.
Market data source: tastytrade DXLink using the active CME NQ futures contract. Normal CME Globex hours are Sunday–Friday 5:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m. CT with a daily 4:00–5:00 p.m. maintenance break. Stale CME-session data is blocked instead of generating a trade signal. This dashboard is an execution aid, not a guarantee of profitability or investment advice.
