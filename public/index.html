MCL V1 is a new independent paper record: hypothetical signals and fills for one 100-barrel MCL contract; contract rolls, commissions, bid/ask, slippage, and unexpected gap losses have not been validated. The stopless design is NOT approved for live trading.
LOCKED OPERATING RULES · V1
  • NYMEX Micro WTI MCL futures, one 100-barrel contract. MCL contract and 1m/daily DXLink candles are independent of the live MNQ engine.
  • Copy MNQ V4's VWAP, opening range, trend-alignment and 1m/5m/15m/30m score thresholds as an unvalidated MCL V1 starting hypothesis.
  • When long, hold through raw WAIT while master score remains above +1. Exit when score is at or below +1. When short, hold below -1 and exit when score is at or above -1.
  • An opposing full BUY/SELL signal reverses immediately. Session closure exits. Trading data errors hold existing paper position and flag a stale feed.
  • There is no fixed take profit or hard stop in MCL V1 paper research. Monitor adverse excursions and design emergency stops before any actual trade.
  • The server runs once per minute and records independent paper positions, raw decision logs, theoretical profits, and push/email signal events; this app NEVER places real orders.
  • Contract rolls, EIA inventory releases, data entitlements, trading costs and drawdowns must be independently validated. Never deploy these stopless research rules for live trading.
Market data source: tastytrade DXLink using the active NYMEX MCL futures contract. Normal NYMEX Globex hours are Sunday–Friday 5:00 p.m.–4:00 p.m. CT with a daily 4:00–5:00 p.m. maintenance break. WTC V1 preserves the existing score-zero hysteresis and -$50 hard stop, removes fixed profit caps entirely, and keeps lifecycle lockout after hard risk exits.
